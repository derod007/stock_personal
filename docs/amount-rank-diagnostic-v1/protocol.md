# Turnover rank universe diagnostic v1

Frozen before outcome computation, 2026-10-23. Read-only exploratory diagnostic, not a trading strategy or independent holdout.

Use only complete paired KRX KOSPI/KOSDAQ daily raw files in the supplied archives, 2023-01-02 through 2024-10-07 (431 sessions). Authenticate archives against original preparation manifest; compare overlapping raw payloads for equality. Validate each raw BAS_DD against filename. Reproduce the previously stored TOP100 membership for every date. Preserve existing name exclusions and amount descending/code ascending tie order; extend to 500. No present-day constituents, scores or later survivor lists. Require both markets and at least 500 eligible records each day. Keep symbol+market identity separate, do not splice transfers.

Fixed bands: ranks 1–100, 101–300, 301–500 after existing exclusions. No lower liquidity threshold optimized from outcomes. Signal at T close; entry benchmark T+1 open; fixed exits T+5/T+10/T+20 close. Exact market sessions, no shifted missing dates. Need positive, finite internally consistent OHLC and positive volume for every future session. All missing, zero-volume/invalid and boundary-short outcomes remain unknown with reasons. Record locked-bar presence (high=low) as an execution ambiguity, not as known unfillable. Also report sensitivity excluding any locked bar in the window. Do not infer tradability from OHLC or turnover alone.

Metrics: raw close return, low-water excursion min(low)/entry-1 and high-water excursion max(high)/entry-1 over full holding window; no intrabar timing ambiguity from stops since none are simulated. These are benchmark price changes, not fills or net returns. No costs, stop, target, leverage, capital or portfolio simulation. Market cap, market board, T turnover and turnover percentiles describe confounding and liquidity only. Corporate actions/price adjustment unverified, never silently correct or remove outliers.

Aggregation: equal weight within band/date, then equal weight dates; report means, pooled median, positive-return share, excursion statistics, row counts and missing counts. Compare each lower band minus TOP100 on shared dates only, return and low-water excursion, with daily difference median and positive fraction. Never assume individual observations are independent. Report full sample, 2023, 2024 partial, and common prior score-study window starting 2023-06-27. Check rank order and source-calendar boundaries.

Repeated-appearance sensitivity: greedily retain a symbol's first TOP500 appearance then next appearance >20 sessions later; selection is based on appearance alone, before future availability, rank band or outcome. Recompute same-day contrasts, disclose reduced sample. Symbol sensitivity: average each lower-band observation's excess over same-day TOP100 within symbol, then equal weight symbols; also show symmetrically TOP100 observations vs lower group. This is a different weighting, not a portfolio.

Interpretation: only a direction persistent across years, horizons and weighting sensitivities merits a new hypothesis. Mixed results are inconclusive. A better raw-return band does not establish net profitable trades, and a worse TOP100 cannot establish the cause of previous score-strategy losses. Do not tune cutoffs or introduce a new entry rule. No inference for unsupplied later all-market data.

Private outputs: all selected dated observations/outcomes, daily band statistics/comparisons, summary, readable report and input/source/protocol hashes. Generic code, protocol, synthetic tests via PR only. Production, original archives and existing accounts untouched.
