# Frozen-entry exit flow comparison

Exploratory research after PR133, not independent validation or operational change. Freeze every PR133 selected feature/bin observation, including unknown future windows. No selecting only eventual +15% winners. Same D+1 open in all arms; no capital, position or sector limit. Overlapping independent trades are not a portfolio.

Use only each original source segment; never stitch prices or extend with another provider. Require valid positive-volume entry candle, known past ATR14 and positive initial stop. ATR14 is simple mean TR over latest14 completed sessions; TR includes previous close. The PR133 signal has at least21 valid past candles. Freeze initial stop=entry open−2*ATR14(D); no tuning. Nonpositive stop => unmodelable in every arm, do not clamp. Entry is modeled at opening price, roundtrip cost multipliers buy1.0015/sell0.9985 as PR133.

Four comparable arms share that initial stop:
- fixed20_risk: initial stop, otherwise D+20 close.
- target15: initial stop or 1.15*entry; no time limit.
- trailing: ratchet stop after every completed session to max(previous stop, highest close since entry−2*currentATR14), active next session only; no time limit/target.
- trend10: initial stop, plus close below minimum low of previous10 sessions (current bar excluded) schedules sale at next session open. At segment end a pending sale is still open, not a known fill.

Processing: existing pending market sale at next open first; otherwise a gap through a stop executes at open; target arm gap above target executes at open; otherwise intraday stop before target when both touched. Entry day is included. Trailing update and trend decision only after current bar survived active orders. Never apply today's new trail to today's earlier low. Initial stop stays fixed for target/trend/time; trailing stop never decreases. Positive volume required for execution/terminal mark. Missing/invalid bar encountered before exit => unknown, never skip/resume through the gap. Zero-volume intermediate valid candle does not itself prove execution; if an order would trigger there, classify unknown. End-of-segment remaining holdings are open, valued at last valid positive-volume close with hypothetical exit costs, not realized profits. Closed positions before later missing data stay closed.

Original PR133 no-stop20 outcome is preserved separately, not relabeled as new fixed20_risk. Report (1) all modeled arm outcomes closed/open/unknown, (2) common terminal-priced entries across four arms, (3) common rows also having known original20 outcome for original-reference comparison. State completion/denominator differences. Valuation horizon differs for variable exits; no annualization. Closed-only mean is supplemental; primary includes open marked positions and separates counts. Date-equal signal-date average primary, trade/symbol equal secondary. Win rate terminal includes marked positions explicitly. Nonoverlap sensitivity uses frozen decision dates before inspecting outcomes: first selected signal per symbol suppresses next20 sessions regardless of actual exit. This is not a capital-respecting account simulation; positions may still overlap after20 sessions.

Do not optimize stop multipliers/lookbacks/target from these results. A useful exit requires positive common date-equal terminal net in each source period and improvement vs common fixed20_risk, with nonoverlap direction checked. No claim maximal possible profit, exact intraday event order, full corporate-action adjustments or verified opening liquidity. No operational settings/journals modified; draft PR only.
